How analysis works

A proper live version should evaluate recent form, home/away performance, goals for and against, head-to-head context, xG when available, injuries/team news and market odds. It should show uncertainty and abstain when data is missing. HT/FT and correct-score markets are inherently difficult to predict reliably.
